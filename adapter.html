Legacy Pneumatic Cylinder Retrofit Adapter

Implemented

Project overview

I designed a custom adapter to accommodate a modern replacement cylinder on legacy equipment with an incompatible piston-rod interface. I validated fit using a 3D-printed prototype before coordinating aluminum machining with an external supplier. The finished adapter was installed and is functional.
