Industrial Cleaning Line Automation

Awaiting approval

Project overview

Developed an automation concept for an industrial cleaning line spanning multiple process routes. I evaluated robotic integration, equipment layout, process standardization and carrier interfaces while coordinating with external engineering teams. I designed five adapter configurations to provide a standardized gripping interface for existing carriers. The proposed system is awaiting management approval.
