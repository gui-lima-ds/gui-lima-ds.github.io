BMW F-Series Coilover Conversion

Final design

Project overview

I designed a low-profile custom aluminum upper strut mount to address fitment challenges during a rear air-suspension-to-coilover conversion. The design was developed in SolidWorks, refined through 3D-printed prototypes and externally machined. The image shows the final CAD design, not a photograph of the manufactured component.
