Centrifugal Pendulum Vibration Absorber

Research in progress

Project overview

My master’s thesis investigates torsional vibration attenuation using centrifugal pendulum vibration absorbers, with an emphasis on end-stop stiffness and damping. I work with an experimental test rig and MATLAB modeling to evaluate absorber behavior. I also designed and 3D-printed a modified accelerometer housing that enables additional weights to be attached to individual absorbers for asymmetry experiments.
