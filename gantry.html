3-Axis Gantry Robotic Gripper

Academic project

Project overview

My freshman engineering team developed a servo-actuated robotic gripper for a three-axis gantry to pick up and stack eight blocks. The gripper was designed in CAD and fabricated from laser-cut components. I also worked on MATLAB-generated G-code and timed pauses to coordinate motion and gripping.
