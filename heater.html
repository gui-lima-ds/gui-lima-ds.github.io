Portable Off-Grid Water Heater

Senior capstone · 2025

Project overview

As part of a four-person senior capstone team, I contributed to a portable battery-powered water heater for outdoor and off-grid applications. My responsibilities included heating element research and design, bill of materials development and engineering design research. The team used SolidWorks, ANSYS thermal analysis and experimental testing. The prototype demonstrated heating capability but did not achieve the intended boiling temperature, identifying opportunities to improve thermal efficiency.
