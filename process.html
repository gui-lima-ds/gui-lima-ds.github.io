Automated Lapping and Polishing Development

In progress

Project overview

This project focuses on evaluating automated lapping and polishing equipment and alternative abrasive technologies to improve manufacturing efficiency, process consistency and production capacity. The investigation compares conventional loose abrasives with fixed-abrasive diamond technologies and explores diamond polishing pads. My responsibilities include coordinating with equipment manufacturers, reviewing proposed methods and assessing integration, throughput, labor utilization, consumable costs and reliability.
